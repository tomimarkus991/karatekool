# Karatekool Nüke

The public website of the Karatekool Nüke karate club, with a members-only area for the training calendar and study materials.

## Language

**Member**:
A person on the Whitelist who signs in with their Google account to use the members-only area.
_Avoid_: User, account

**Admin**:
A Member marked as an Admin on the Whitelist, who may manage the club's calendar.
_Avoid_: Coach (as a role), superuser

**Whitelist**:
The list, maintained by the club in the repository, of email addresses allowed to sign in and which of them are Admins.
_Avoid_: Allowlist, invite list

**Group**:
A training group of the club, identified by a letter.
_Avoid_: Class, level

**Event**:
Anything on the club's calendar: a Training, an All-day event or a Multi-day event.
_Avoid_: Entry, item

**Training**:
An Event at a set time for one or more Groups.
_Avoid_: Normal event, class, session

**All-day event**:
An Event that fills a single day with a title and subtitle but no time.

**Multi-day event**:
An Event with a title that spans several consecutive days.

**Preset**:
A saved Training or Multi-day event an Admin reuses to create new ones quickly.
_Avoid_: Template

**Highlight**:
Visual emphasis an Admin puts on a calendar event's time or on one of its Groups to catch the eye; it carries no other meaning.
_Avoid_: Important, priority

**Application**:
A request to join the club, either for a child (submitted by a Guardian) or for an adult.
_Avoid_: Sign-up, registration

**Guardian**:
The parent or carer who submits an Application on behalf of a child.
_Avoid_: Parent, info contact

**Question**:
A message a visitor sends to the club through the website.
_Avoid_: Inquiry, contact request
